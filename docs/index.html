UFODAW is a small, free digital audio workstation for Windows. Audio and MIDI tracks, VST3 plugins, a sampler, a drum rack and deep automation, in a warm dark interface that stays out of your way.

Everything you need to finish a song

Audio tracks

Import wav, aiff, flac, mp3 and ogg by dragging them in. Record several armed tracks at once, each from its own input, with live monitoring through the track's effects, and watch the waveform grow while you record.

MIDI & synth

Piano roll with velocity editing, quantize, swing and humanize, plus a scale and chord helper. A built-in polyphonic synth with ADSR and low-pass, or any VST3 instrument. Map your controller with MIDI learn.

Drum rack & sampler

16 pads, each a small sampler with velocity layers and round-robin. A Simpler-style sampler with slicing by hits, slices to MIDI, and BPM detection.

Loop recording & comping

Every loop pass becomes a take. Punch in and out, comp the best parts together in the comp view, and drag takes as blocks.

Mixer & effects

A 6-band parametric EQ with a live curve and spectrum, a compressor with sidechain, soft knee and parallel mix, a gate, de-esser, multiband compressor, saturation, chorus/flanger, ping-pong delay, 8-line reverb and a lookahead limiter on every track, all in one tidy effects chain. Plus buses and sends, a master channel, and plugin delay compensation of up to two seconds.

Automation

Breakpoint envelopes for volume, pan, every built-in effect and any plugin parameter, with automation rides, takes and comping.

Warp & stretch

Warp audio clips to the project's beat grid, time-stretch and pitch-shift powered by Rubber Band, with clip gain, fades and crossfades.

Metering & mastering aids

LUFS loudness meter, clipping lamps on every meter, a reference-track A/B switch, and a CPU readout so you see trouble before it crackles.

Scripting with Lua

Write small scripts that make chord progressions, drum patterns and melodies, humanize clips or set up a project, with a built-in music library. They run in a safe sandbox and are one undo step.

A much better synth, piano and drums

Unison, filters with their own envelopes, an LFO and glide; a piano engine; 808 and legato basses; remade drums with soft and round-robin hits, and two new kits. 43 ready-made sounds.

Clip launcher

A grid of clips to start live: a column per track, a row per idea. Everything starts on the next bar or beat, so it stays in time. Fill the cells with patterns, audio files or clips from your arrangement.

Updates & problem reports

One click on Install now updates the program (checked against GitHub's checksum). Report a problem and the notice after a crash show a plain-text report that you send yourself; nothing is sent automatically.

Multi-core & crash-proof

Heavy projects are mixed on several CPU cores at once, with exactly the same sound as on one core. A plugin that crashes, even in its own window, is caught and blocked instead of taking the program down.

Sounds & templates included

A starter pack of drums, bass, synth sounds and loops made by the program itself (free to use), five drum kits, ten synth sounds, seven ready-made project templates and a sample browser with preview, so you can make a beat in the first minute.

Freeze & groups

Freeze a heavy track to audio and switch its plugins off to save CPU, and fold tracks into named groups (folders) that collapse and mute or solo together.

A safety net for plugins

Plugins are scanned in a helper process, and a plugin that crashes while playing is caught and switched off, so the program and your session keep going.

Hardware friendly

MIDI clock, external analog synths, controller support, sequencer plugins like Stochas driving any instrument, and a built-in chromatic tuner.

Built around how you actually work

  • Command palette. Press Ctrl+K and type what you want to do.
  • Your own shortcuts. Every command can be rebound.
  • Project tabs. Several projects open at once, with a clipboard shared between them.
  • Arrangement sections. Name verse, chorus and bridge, and navigate by them.
  • Safe by default. Autosave and crash recovery, time-stamped backups on every save, and a version history.
  • Move your project anywhere. Save with all audio in one folder and take it to another disk or PC.
  • Export your way. WAV 16/24/32-bit float and more, plus stem export with every track as its own file.
  • Quick tour. A short guided tour on first launch gets you playing quickly.

What's new

0.9.3.1 latest

  • Double-clicking a project opens the newest version: the "Open with > Always use this app" choice of Windows kept pointing at the copy of UFODAW that was chosen once, so an older version could open your projects. UFODAW (and the installer) now point it at the version that is started or installed. Tests check that projects from the older formats still open.

0.9.3

  • Scripting with Lua: File > Scripts (Lua) runs scripts from your scripts folder. They can add tracks, write and change MIDI notes, set up the synth, change the tempo and render the project, with a music library (note names, scales, chords, euclidean rhythms). A settings dialog for each script, one undo step per run, a safe sandbox with time and memory limits. Six example scripts are included.
  • Command line: UFODAW.exe --run-script script.lua [project.mdaw] runs a script without a dialog.

0.9.2

  • New synth: up to five unison oscillators, a second and a sub oscillator, a resonant filter with its own envelope, an LFO, glide, a pitch envelope and mono legato mode. Edit sound... in the piano roll opens every setting.
  • Piano engine with six pianos, and bass sounds such as 808 sub, Moog bass and Legato bass: 43 sounds in all, balanced to the same loudness.
  • Remade drums: better kicks, snares, claps, hats and cymbals; soft and round-robin versions of every kit sound as velocity layers; two new kits, 909 and Acoustic (with a small room and stereo).
  • Command line: UFODAW.exe --render project.mdaw out.wav renders a project to an audio file (wav, flac, aiff, mp3 or ogg) without opening a dialog.

0.9.1

  • Clip launcher: a grid of clips (a column per track, a row per idea) that you start live; everything starts on the next bar or beat. Cells are saved in the project; a new Launcher jam template shows it.
  • Project check: one dialog for everything that did not come through when a project opens, with Find the files... to look for missing audio in a folder. Projects now record their format version.
  • Voice character: the pitch shift of a clip can keep the formants, so a vocal does not turn into a chipmunk.
  • Automatic tests on every change: the engine gives identical results on one core and on several, and old, new and damaged project files keep opening.

0.9.0

  • Install now: the Update available button installs the new version for you: it downloads the installer, checks the checksum, updates and starts UFODAW again.
  • Problem reports: File > Report a problem, and a report offered after a crash (version, Windows, processor, the crashed file; no project data). You send it yourself, by a prefilled GitHub issue or by copying it.
  • Documentation: a Getting started section, a section on updates and reports, and a troubleshooting section in the guide.

0.8.9

  • Multi-core audio: tracks and buses are rendered side by side on several CPU cores (bit-identical to single-core), set under Audio settings > CPU; automatic by default.
  • Plugin crash isolation: a crash inside a plugin's own window or callbacks is caught, its windows close, the plugin is blocked and named. If the program is closed anyway, that plugin is blocked at the next start with a notice.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. UFODAW is free software under the GNU AGPLv3. You can use it, study it and modify it.

Does it run on macOS or Linux?

Not yet. UFODAW is built and tested for Windows. The code has a macOS build that can also host Audio Units, but that is untested because it needs a Mac. There is no Linux build.

Can I use my audio interface's ASIO driver?

Yes. ASIO is supported when built with Steinberg's ASIO SDK. Otherwise WASAPI and DirectSound drivers are available, or an aggregate driver from your interface maker.

Which plugins work?

VST3 instruments and effects, with their own editor windows. Plugin state is stored in the project, and what you do in a plugin window is part of undo. VST2 and CLAP are not supported.

How does it compare to REAPER, FL Studio or Ableton Live?

UFODAW is free, open and light, but it is young and Windows-only, with fewer built-in sounds and effects. See the honest comparison.

Can I build it myself?

Yes. It is C++17 with CMake and JUCE 8: cmake -S . -B build, then cmake --build build --config Release. Use Release, since Debug is too slow for audio.
