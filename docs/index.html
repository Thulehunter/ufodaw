UFODAW is a small, free digital audio workstation for Windows. Audio and MIDI tracks, VST3 plugins, a sampler, a drum rack and deep automation, in a warm dark interface that stays out of your way.

Everything you need to finish a song

Audio tracks

Import wav, aiff, flac, mp3 and ogg by dragging them in. Record several armed tracks at once, each from its own input, with live monitoring through the track's effects.

MIDI & synth

Piano roll with velocity editing, quantize, swing and humanize. A built-in polyphonic synth with ADSR and low-pass, or any VST3 instrument.

Drum rack & sampler

16 pads, each a small sampler with velocity layers and round-robin. A Simpler-style sampler with slicing by hits, slices to MIDI, and BPM detection.

Loop recording & comping

Every loop pass becomes a take. Punch in and out, comp the best parts together in the comp view, and drag takes as blocks.

Mixer & effects

EQ, compressor with sidechain, delay and reverb on every track, plus buses and sends, a master channel, and plugin delay compensation of up to two seconds.

Automation

Breakpoint envelopes for volume, pan, every built-in effect and any plugin parameter, with automation rides, takes and comping.

Warp & stretch

Warp audio clips to the project's beat grid, time-stretch and pitch-shift powered by Rubber Band, with clip gain, fades and crossfades.

Metering & mastering aids

LUFS loudness meter, clipping lamps on every meter, a reference-track A/B switch, and a CPU readout so you see trouble before it crackles.

Hardware friendly

MIDI clock, external analog synths, controller support, sequencer plugins like Stochas driving any instrument, and a built-in chromatic tuner.

Built around how you actually work

  • Command palette. Press Ctrl+K and type what you want to do.
  • Your own shortcuts. Every command can be rebound.
  • Project tabs. Several projects open at once, with a clipboard shared between them.
  • Arrangement sections. Name verse, chorus and bridge, and navigate by them.
  • Safe by default. Autosave and crash recovery, time-stamped backups on every save, and a version history.
  • Move your project anywhere. Save with all audio in one folder and take it to another disk or PC.
  • Export your way. WAV 16/24/32-bit float and more, plus stem export with every track as its own file.
  • Quick tour. A short guided tour on first launch gets you playing quickly.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. UFODAW is free software under the GNU AGPLv3. You can use it, study it and modify it.

Does it run on macOS or Linux?

Not yet. UFODAW is built and tested for Windows. The code has a macOS build that can also host Audio Units, but that is untested because it needs a Mac. There is no Linux build.

Can I use my audio interface's ASIO driver?

Yes. ASIO is supported when built with Steinberg's ASIO SDK. Otherwise WASAPI and DirectSound drivers are available, or an aggregate driver from your interface maker.

Which plugins work?

VST3 instruments and effects, with their own editor windows. Plugin state is stored in the project, and what you do in a plugin window is part of undo. VST2 and CLAP are not supported.

Can I build it myself?

Yes. It is C++17 with CMake and JUCE 8: cmake -S . -B build, then cmake --build build --config Release. Use Release, since Debug is too slow for audio.
